Model logic follows the revised GOE plant economic model (Revised Model GOE 08 Nov 2026, Base Case v4, with the Litus options added Nov 9, 2026; HCl make up 0.18 m3/h; capex US$9.12M per module at USD/CAD 1.40): modules = site brine flow ÷ brine per module, rounded up, built in batches of 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 modules at months 1, 4, 7, 10, 14 and then 6 every 4 months from Jan 2027 (see Build schedule); production is capped at the site flow, so the last module can run part loaded; 95% DLE recovery and 95% uptime; income tax at the rate set above with 7 year straight line depreciation and loss carry forward; maintenance at the financial model rate (PE CAPEX Forecast R1, a share of capex per year); Litus royalty switches from the BPO to the APO rate the month after the partner's cumulative after tax cash turns positive (model Inputs C52, C53, set by the option in Inputs L44); the licence fee is a one time opex in month 1 (Inputs C55); the marketing fee is Inputs C56; acid, caustic, water and power fixed per column cycle (so $/t falls as ppm rises); above the GOE design loading the media and regeneration use are increased. Downstream lithium losses shown in the GOE mass balance are not included. NPV uses monthly cash flows discounted to the build start. Option 1: Litus funds and owns the plant and pays the brine owner a royalty; the plant still pays the media lease, as in the Excel files. Option 2: the partner funds and owns the plant and pays Litus the royalty, licence fee and media lease. Below the low concentration threshold the media lease uses the lower rate and NaOH is scaled by ppm ÷ threshold (GOE Media Requirements F37, F38).

This is Litus Financial Model V11 Fixed v3 itself: every sheet and formula of the Excel file runs in this page, so loans, debt, interest, grants, pilots, testing, battery recycling, people, tax and the GOE v4 plant model give the same numbers as the file. Change any input and the whole model recalculates. Your changes are saved in your workspace; the Excel file is not changed.
